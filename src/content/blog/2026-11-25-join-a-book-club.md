Like most people, who carry around little attention sucking bricks in their pockets these days, I too suffer from a short attention span. I've been fully Instagram Reels pilled - it's a good product - to the chagrin of my former book loving self. 

Which is ironic, since I run a book club. You'd think that a book club is for book readers discussing books that they have read, but for me it actually provides the driving force to read books in the first place.

I'm the kind of person who tends more to do things if there's someone chasing me for it, and I have pretty good feeling that I'm not alone here. For most of us, school and work (and church, for some) provide most of the thrust to do the things that we want to do. But outside of these venues - absent external motivation - it's quite easy to slack off and not do anything.

So I find the book club useful for getting me to read in the first place, and I think others do too. You don't have to already a book reader to join one. Find your external motivation.
