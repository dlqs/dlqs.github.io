The case of [Altantuyaa Shaariibuu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Murder_of_Shaariibuugiin_Altantuyaa) is just so fascinating. To me it's a little window into the wild world of Malaysian politics, where an event like this is but a blip in the background of someone as important as the Prime Minister of Malaysia.

To quickly summarize: Altantuya was a Mongolian model/translator who back in 2006 worked for one of Najib's close associates, back when Najib was only Deputy PM. Until one day she was found _blown up_ in a Kuala Lumpar parking lot with C4, by Najib's police bodyguards. This case has so many twists and turns and till this day it is not known _why_ she was murdered. 

I hope that one day they finally resolve it and we can see a movie about this.
