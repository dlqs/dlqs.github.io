1. Our Father, who art in heaven
2. Hallowed be thy name
3. Thy kingdom come
4. Thy will be done
5. On earth as it is in heaven
6. Give us this our daily bread
7. And forgive us for those we trespass
8. As we forgive those who trespass against us
9. And lead us not into tempation
10. But deliver us from evil
11. Amen.
