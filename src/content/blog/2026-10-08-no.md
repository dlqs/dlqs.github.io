A gradual realization everyone has as they grow up is the fact that there is, in fact, no one in charge. If you find in the position of asking who's in charge, more often than not, its you.

I'd guess that for most people they make this observation first with their parents. Parents, as they are, come with a sense of invincibility. But as you grow up, you start seeing cracks.
Of knowledge, of ability. Until the cracks grow into chasms, then you wonder how did they ever make it out of _their_ childhood.

Parenting is hard, and most parents are just "figuring it out", just like most people in most endavors. I find that I have to constantly remind myself about this, especially when I'm at new events meeting people.
