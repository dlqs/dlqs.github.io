Something that I recently realized is that there are good dentists and bad dentists. That "dentist" is just a binary label; within dentists, like in most disciplines, there is a whole quality spectrum.

I found that out when I switched over to my new, current dentist, who informed me that I was supposed to get bitewing x-rays (the cannon looking one) once a year. My previous dentist did not. So I had not done one in a long time, and when I did, we discovered a hidden decay between two of my molars that had eluded previous visual inspections.

It can be scary to worry about the variance in quality between individual providers, when we _cannot_ pick them, say like your pilot, or your surgeon. But I'm sure that e.g. pilots who graduated at the tenth percentile fly all the time, and I'm sure I've taken one of those flights before, and we're all fine. Perhaps this is testament to the pilot qualification system? Either way, I'm grateful that these systems exist.
