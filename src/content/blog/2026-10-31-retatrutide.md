The weight loss drug revolution continues!

First we had single receptor, GLP-1 agnostists (Semaglutide, "Ozempic"). Then we had double receptor, GLP-1 _and_ GIP agonists (Tirzepatide). Now we have triple receptor agonists: GLP-1, GIP and glucagon (Retatrutide). And they're better than ever!

I don't claim to be an expert here, but my very simplified, high level understanding is:
 - GLP-1 agonists help you lose weight, by reducing your appetite
 - GIP agonists help you to regulate blood sugar, and somehow enhances the weight loss effect of GLP-1
 - Glucagon agonists help you burn more calories

So, with retatrutide, we have both decreased energy intake and increased energy expenditure. Great!

Even though these drugs are in varying stages of medical approval, the headline numbers seem quite promising: semaglutide already helps reduce weight by about 15% after 68 weeks, while retatrutide goes even further up to 25%. Besides that, there are a bunch of promising-but-not-fully-checked benefits such as: reduced risk of Alzheimer's, reduction of knee arthritis pain, better cardio health, etc. 

The miracle drug is upon us.
